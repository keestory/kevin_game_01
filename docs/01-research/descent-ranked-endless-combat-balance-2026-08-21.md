## Internal hypothesis

Telegraphed attacking enemies, multi-hit priority targets, and craft-specific strengths and costs will increase meaningful lane changes and voluntary retries without making first-run failure feel arbitrary.

This remains an internal hypothesis. The implementation is a local validation slice, not evidence of App Store rank or D1/D7 improvement.

## Red-team stop criteria

- An enemy projectile damages before a readable warning or leaves no escape lane.
- Brute time-to-kill is perceived as stalled rather than tactical by at least 2 of 5 observed users.
- One craft exceeds the public-board fairness threshold.
- Electric or wind obscures the hostile warning or danger line.
- Intentional low scoring improves expected survival or rank.
- Ten-minute worst-case play breaches entity caps, deterministic checksum, or the 30 fps hard floor.
